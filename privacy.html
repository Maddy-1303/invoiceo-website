Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information Madcreations (“we”) handles when you use the Invoiceo app and this website. If you have a question about it, write to madhanprasat2002r@gmail.com.

The Invoiceo app

  • Your business data stays on your computer. The customers, products, invoices, payments, settings and backups you create are stored on the computer where Invoiceo is installed. We do not receive a copy.
  • Anonymous usage counts. So that we know how many people use Invoiceo, the app (from version 1.0.4) sends us a short message when it is first opened, once a day while it is used, and once when the first invoice is created. Each message carries only a random ID made for this purpose, the app version and the operating system (Windows, macOS or Linux). It never includes your customers, products, invoices, amounts or company details. The messages go to a small server we run on Cloudflare, which does not store IP addresses; Cloudflare’s handling is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. You can turn this off in the app under Settings → Software Info.
  • Update check. Once a day, when you are online, the app asks GitHub whether a newer version of Invoiceo exists; that is an ordinary web request, so GitHub sees your IP address. Apart from this and the usage counts, the app goes online only when you click a link that opens your web browser.
  • No online account. The app’s usernames and passwords exist only on your computer.
  • No advertising and no selling of data, ever.
  • Links you choose to open. Some buttons in the app open a web page in your browser (for example this website, our forms or Buy Me a Coffee). What happens on those pages is covered below or by that site’s own policy.
  • Backups and PDFs. Files you export or share are handled by you; we have no access to them.

This website

  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Installers are downloaded from GitHub as well.
  • Fonts. The text font (Manrope) is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser fetches it from Google’s servers when a page opens. Google’s handling of this is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Forms. Our support, customization and feedback forms are Google Forms. What you type into them (such as your name, email, phone number and message) is sent to our Google account so that we can reply. Google’s handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Analytics. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and download buttons are used. It sets cookies in your browser for this. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses, and we have turned off its advertising features. We use it only to improve the site. You can block these cookies in your browser settings.
  • Email and WhatsApp. If you contact us, we keep the conversation so that we can help you and follow up.

How we use what you send us

Only to answer you, to provide support, to prepare and deliver customization work you asked for, and to improve Invoiceo. We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it

We keep form replies and messages for as long as they are useful for supporting you, and delete them when you ask us to, unless we must keep a record by law (for example, invoices for paid work).

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to madhanprasat2002r@gmail.com.

Children

Invoiceo and this website are meant for businesses and are not aimed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

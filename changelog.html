Version 1.0.0 Latest

9 October 2026

The first release under the Invoiceo name.

  • New name, logo and a clean light design.
  • Tamil added as an app language, alongside English, Hindi, Nepali, French, Spanish, Chinese and Tibetan (partly translated).
  • Two screen layouts to choose from in Settings: Standard and the new Modern layout, with a grouped sidebar, a top bar with a quick “new invoice, quotation or receipt” button, and a new dashboard.
  • Thermal receipts with Tamil and other Indian-language text now print the same way they look in the preview.
  • Receipts can print automatically after each invoice is created (Modern layout).
  • In the Modern layout, an unfinished invoice can be saved as a draft and continued later from the Invoices list.
  • Barcode scanning on the New Invoice screen is more reliable.
  • In the Modern layout, Products and Services have their own pages, each with its own columns and filters, and the Customers page shows what each customer still owes.
  • The app tells you when a newer version is out (it checks once a day while you are online; billing never needs the internet).
